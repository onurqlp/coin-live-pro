# UZAYX 200 — Strategy-first product and minimal interface / staging plan
Date 2026-10-20. Status: DESIGN STAGED, NOT IMPLEMENTED, NOT DEPLOYED.

## Evidence baseline
Existing UZAYX web/uzayx/site.html includes market dashboard, TURAN 30, 200 AR-GE families, ATLAS, V4 X1-X6, leverage scenario lab, legacy cohorts, virtual funds, support/proof and system health. These should not all be on the daily home screen. Prior 2026-10-18 handover: ALTI A1/A2/A3/B1/B2/T0 are different source-eligibility classes, currently no claim of live verified profitability; COIN.LIVE original seven strategies/ledger and 10k virtual bankroll must not be overwritten. Preserve separate research tickets and paper ledger.
## Strategy hierarchy
200+ means registry capacity/target, NOT 200 independently validated alpha sources. Tag each as IDEA, DATA_BLOCKED, BACKTESTED, OOS_VERIFIED, FORWARD_PAPER, PROMOTED, RETIRED. No automatically promoted strategies merely from positive backtests. ALTI missing access stays missing, not magically tradable.
Canonical strategy schema: strategy_id, family_id, version, features, venue, required_source, rules, market_regime, coverage, correlations, research_history, oos_metrics, forward_metrics, approved_allocation_cap, last_test_at, decision_trace. Make strategy dependency graph to deduplicate shared input effects; nominal strategy votes != independent evidence.
## Single decision pipeline
point-in-time source registry -> event detection -> strategy independent assessments and conflicts -> volatility/regime gating -> source freshness and trust -> risk-adjusted net expectancy rank -> shared budget allocation proposal -> paper order intent -> fill validation -> margin reserve and open position -> mark/fee/funding/exit -> event ledger -> evaluation. Denied/expired candidate persists for research. Do not make every bubble an automatic filled order.
Score and capital sizing should use empirical calibration, net-of-fee edge, tail risk, effective diversification and uncertainty. Drawdown/correlation and illiquidity cap allocations. Reserve spare margin. Fail closed for stale quotes and unavailable short access. Do not estimate precise win probability from arbitrary indicator count.
## Position+treasury
Always visible on home: equity, available cash, locked margin, open notional, open trades, realized/unrealized net PNL, source stale warning. Each trade drilldown: venue, symbol, direction, leverage, margin, notional, entry VWAP, current mark/time, net fees, funding, realized/unrealized PNL, ROE, stop/TP, estimated liquidation when model credible, entry and exit reason, supporting/opposing strategies, lifecycle timeline. Open position list must not require navigating away from home. Keep legacy V3, main V4, research 10USDT pilot and new treasury distinct until reconciled migration.
## Historical evaluation
Recreate all eligible candidates, not only officially signaled positions. Output separate cohorts: issued signals; unsigned setups; virtual entries; unfilled intents; open trades; closed trades; hindsight-only analysis. All past information only as of candidate decision timestamp; replay future path for exits, never for selection. Sensitivity to latency, bid-ask spread, maker/taker tier, leverage, margin mode, funding, liquidity, split of fills, same-bar TP/stop priority. Compute net PNL, drawdown, MAE, MFE, exposure efficiency, survival/delisting risk, sample size and uncertainty intervals. Candidate with no source evidence is DATA_UNAVAILABLE, not profitable or losing. Include 10x min economically viable ticket check.
## News, manipulation and learning
Continuous curated ingestion as scheduling permits, not claim all-internet instant coverage. Quote and event provenance: exchange/feed, observed_at/effective_at/ingested_at, TTL, lag, URL/source id, archive hash. Event features: surge in spot/perp volume, OI, funding, liquidations, trade pressure, microstructure, listing news, token unlocks, abnormal social velocity and retracement. Distinguish anomaly indicator from proven wrongdoing. Stratify historic event research and post-spike SHORT failures, avoid selective survivor sample. Maintain model versioned experiments; no on-the-fly strategy changes without out-of-sample gate and audit.
## UI information architecture
Home = 1) total paper equity, cash available, margin in use, net PNL 2) all open positions with progressive detail 3) Top 3 opportunities ranked with supporting independent evidence, directional conflict & net risk 4) urgent source/risk alerts only. Radar = ranked opportunities, bubble size tied to grounded conviction and liquidity not mere agreement count. Strategies = performance leaderboard, family/filter, 200+ full registry deep view. Archive = ATLAS, old cohorts, X1-X6, research events, signed/unsigned candidates, raw proofs. System = status and operator diagnostics; only incident badge visible during normal operation.
Mobile navigation 4–5 items: Home, Radar, Kasa, Strateji, More. Show numerical risk facts, avoid gambling-like success claims. Progressive disclosures and responsive cards at 360/390px, no horizontal overflow, accessible labels, tap targets. Default hides technical UTC telemetry, repeated explanations, old trial calculators, export data and research raw traces without deleting it.
